# Sourcegraph Cody - Research Notes

## Free Credits on Signup
- No free credits offered on signup for Cody itself
- Note: In mid-2025, Sourcegraph transitioned from Cody to a new product called "Amp", offering $10-$40 in free Amp credits for existing Cody users transitioning

## Models Offered
- Claude 3.5 Sonnet (default)
- Claude 3 Haiku
- Mixtral
- Gemini 1.5
- GPT-4o
- Claude 3 Opus (Pro tier)
- StarCoder (for code completions)

## Free Tier Features
- **Autocomplete**: Unlimited code completions
- **Chat/Commands**: 200 chats and commands per month
- **Local Context**: Indexes open project for context awareness
- **Multi-model Support**: Choose from multiple models including Claude 3.5 Sonnet
- **No credit card required**: Free signup with Sourcegraph account

## Trial Duration
- No traditional trial duration
- Free tier is available indefinitely
- Pro tier was previously $9/month but some 2026 sources indicate it may now be free with expanded features
- Enterprise starts at $19-$59/user/month with no free trial (contact sales)
