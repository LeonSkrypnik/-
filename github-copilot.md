# GitHub Copilot Research

## Free Credits on Signup
- **Copilot Free**: Up to 2,000 inline suggestions and 50 premium requests per month at signup - no payment required
- **30-day Free Trial**: New users can get a one-time 30-day trial of Copilot Pro (includes 300 premium requests)
- **Copilot Student**: Unlimited completions and 300 premium requests per month for verified students - free
- **Teachers & OSS Maintainers**: Free access to Copilot Pro for verified teachers and popular open source project maintainers

## Models Offered
- **Copilot Free**: Access to included models for inline suggestions
- **Copilot Pro**: Access to premium models in Copilot Chat, includes Claude Sonnet, GPT-4o and other models
- **Copilot Pro+**: Full access to all available models including latest GPT-5 variants, Claude Opus, Gemini models

## Free Tier Features
- **Copilot Free**:
  - Up to 2,000 code completions per month in IDEs (VS Code, JetBrains, Neovim, Eclipse, Xcode)
  - Up to 50 premium requests (Copilot Chat) per month
  - Multi-language support
  - Context-aware suggestions
  - Intended for individual use only

## Trial Duration
- **Copilot Pro Trial**: 30 days
  - Requires payment method (will auto-convert to paid unless cancelled)
  - Maximum 3 free trials per payment method
  - After trial: $10/month for Copilot Pro
